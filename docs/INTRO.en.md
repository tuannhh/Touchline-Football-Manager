# Touchline — your club, your decisions

[Tiếng Việt](../README.md) · [English](INTRO.en.md) · [Español](INTRO.es.md) · [Português](INTRO.pt.md) · [Français](INTRO.fr.md)

Touchline is an independent football management game for playing on your own computer. Build a squad, shape your tactics, negotiate transfers and contracts, manage your staff, and follow matches on a 2D pitch. Save separate careers and return to them whenever you like.

The current database covers clubs across three league levels in England, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Portugal, the Netherlands and Vietnam, alongside simulated UEFA competitions and international call-ups. Features include drag-and-drop line-ups, in-possession and out-of-possession formations, player profiles and available real portraits, injuries and morale, AI club transfers, and a built-in editor for experimenting with finances and attributes.

The interface supports Vietnamese, English, French, Spanish and Portuguese, with selectable currencies and measurement units. After installation, the game runs locally in your browser without Steam or an account; downloaded assets work offline. This is a single-player project, not an online multiplayer service. Some source data remains incomplete, and skills, finances, schedules and results are simulated.
