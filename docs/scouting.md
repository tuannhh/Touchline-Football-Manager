# Scouting in Touchline

Scouting turns the club's scouts into a source of recruitment recommendations. It uses the players and staff in the current career; it does not fetch new real-world scouting reports or sign players automatically.

## Request a search

Open **Scouting** and create an assignment for a position you want to strengthen, or ask for young prospects. Set the maximum age, estimated transfer fee and expected weekly wage for a new contract, then choose an eligible scout, sporting director or assistant manager and a duration of one to four weeks. Up to three assignments may run at once, with one assignment per staff member. The positional search uses detailed football positions, including full-backs, defensive midfielders, attacking midfielders and wingers.

Assignments progress as the career advances. Active work and completed reports remain visible on the scouting page. Reports identify the scout, search criteria and observation date, so you can distinguish an older recommendation from a new one.

## Recommendations and decisions

A report gives the manager up to five candidates to review, with estimates of current ability and potential, transfer information and the reason each player was recommended. The player's profile and the usual transfer negotiation workflow remain available from the recommendation.

Ability and potential assessments are uncertain: scout quality and time spent on the assignment influence their accuracy. A report is a snapshot of the observation date, not a promise about a player's future development, final transfer fee or contract demands. The player's situation may change afterwards.

Recommendations do not automatically purchase a player or add them to the manager's shortlist. Financial feasibility is advisory at the report stage. Actual negotiations still check the selling club, player demands, transfer and wage budgets, cash reserves and the financial controls applying to the club when the deal is completed.

## Routine scouting

When scouting responsibility is assigned to a staff member and there is no active assignment, the scouting team can send weekly recommendations of players aged 23 or younger automatically. This gives the manager ideas without requiring a new search every week. Manual assignments take priority over the routine search. Time is measured using dates in the career rather than the number of clicks on Continue.

Existing careers gain the scouting system when loaded. Scouting records are included in saved games; recommendations use the career's own player and transfer state, including later AI transfers.
